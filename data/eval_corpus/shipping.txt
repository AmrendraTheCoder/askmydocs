SHIPPING AND LOGISTICS — INTERNAL REFERENCE

CARRIER ALLOCATION
Orders are allocated to a carrier by pincode serviceability, then by cost. A pincode served by only one partner skips the cost comparison entirely. Allocation runs at pickup time, not at order time, so a carrier outage between the two does not strand the parcel.

PACKAGING
Fragile items ship in double-walled cartons with corner protection. Liquids over 500ml require a sealed inner bag regardless of carton type. Packaging weight counts toward the billed weight, which is why oversized cartons quietly increase shipping cost.

VOLUMETRIC WEIGHT
Carriers bill on whichever is greater, actual weight or volumetric weight. Volumetric weight is length times width times height in centimetres divided by 5000. A light but bulky parcel is therefore billed as if it were heavy.

TRACKING
A tracking number is issued at manifest, not at pickup, so a number can exist for several hours before it shows any scan. Customers frequently report this as a broken tracking link when it is simply a parcel that has not been scanned yet.

FAILED DELIVERY
A courier attempts delivery three times before returning the parcel to origin. Each attempt must be logged with a reason code. Parcels returned to origin are restocked within 5 working days of arriving back at the warehouse.

ADDRESS CORRECTION
An address can be corrected while the parcel is in transit only if the new address falls within the same city. Cross-city redirection is not supported and the parcel must complete its return to origin first.

REVERSE PICKUP
Reverse pickups are scheduled within 48 hours of approval and attempted twice. If both attempts fail the customer is asked to self-ship and is reimbursed up to 150 rupees against a receipt.

SHIPPING ERROR CODES
Error code SHP-1140 means the pincode is serviceable but the carrier has paused intake at that facility.
Error code SHP-2250 means the declared value exceeds the carrier insurance limit for that lane.
Error code SHP-3360 means the parcel dimensions were rejected as oversized at the sorting hub.

INTERNATIONAL
International orders require a commercial invoice and an HS code per line item. Duties are payable by the recipient unless the order was placed under delivered-duty-paid terms. Customs holds are outside our control and typically clear within 4 working days.

SLA AND PENALTIES
Carriers owe a service credit when they miss the committed transit time on more than 2 percent of monthly volume. Claims must be filed within 30 days of the delivery date, which is why late claims are the most common reason a valid penalty goes uncollected.
